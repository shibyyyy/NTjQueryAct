Jujutsu Sorcerers

Individuals trained to control the flow of cursed energy within their bodies. They use this energy to perform cursed techniques and exorcise curses.

Cursed Spirits

Monstrous beings born from the accumulation of negative human emotions. The strongest among them possess high intelligence and devastating innate techniques.

Domain Expansion

The pinnacle of jujutsu sorcery. A technique that constructs the user's innate domain using cursed energy, trapping the target within a guaranteed-hit environment.
